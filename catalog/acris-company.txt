ACRIS company profile

Plugin portfolio:
- Plugins are published under the vendor name ACRIS E-Commerce GmbH. Technical names start with "Acris" (for example AcrisSearch, AcrisTax, AcrisWishlist). The Proxa plugins (for example ProxaAgeVerification) also belong to ACRIS and are not third-party products.
- Plugin types: Essential, Professional and Premium.
- Categories: Administration & processes, B2B & personalization, Content & shopping experiences, Products & categories, Law & taxes, Registration & checkout, Sales & promotions, SEO & internationalization, Search & navigation, Theme & UX.
- Use cases: optimize B2B processes, increase conversion, improve the shopping experience, increase revenue, increase legal certainty, increase visibility.
- Most plugins are offered as a monthly or yearly subscription in the Shopware Store.

Versioning convention for ACRIS plugins: Shopware 6.7 versions use the 2.x line, Shopware 6.6 versions use 1.x, Shopware 6.5 versions use 0.x.
